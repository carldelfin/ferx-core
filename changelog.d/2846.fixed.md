- A FOCEI fit with an explicit ODE stepper (`rk45`, `vern7`, or `auto`) and the analytic
  gradient now warns (`W_ODE_EXPLICIT_UNSTABLE_TANGENTS`) when the stepper stepped past the
  stability limit of a fast mode that only the η-sensitivities excite, which left the
  predictions clean but the analytic Jacobian, the determinant term and the OFV inflated. The
  fit re-scores each subject with a finite-difference Jacobian once at the end and warns when
  a subject's objective moves by 1.0 or more; the warning names `ode_method = rodas5p` or a
  tighter `ode_reltol` as the fix. This detects the problem and does not change any estimate,
  OFV or step (#2846).
